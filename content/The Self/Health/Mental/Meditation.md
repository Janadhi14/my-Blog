## Meditation
Meditation is one of the core components of buddhism. It is used to cultivate mental clarity, emotional resilience and ultimately enlightening or liberation from suffering 
- This practice doesn't necessarily have to be tied into religion and in my opinion it is rather better off practiced without any ties to religion.
- The approach differs from person to person however the more you do it the better you become. 
- It is not about attaining peace, you will 

## Technique 1
- Do this in the morning for an hour
- Just Sit, Do nothing 
	- Don't focus on anything not even the breath, Let your mind chatter and observe this. 
- All these things will arise from a lifetime of being buried in your mind, fears anxieties, words, imagery. once your mind can go through all of these and resolve them, then you will gain some clarity and you will hit an "empty mental inbox".
