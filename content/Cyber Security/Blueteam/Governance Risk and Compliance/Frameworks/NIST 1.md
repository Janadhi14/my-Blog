NIST stands for National Institute of Standards and Technology 
This is an organisation that provides frameworks for IT based topics.


They are the creators of the NIST Cyber security framework which has had multiple iterations

The NIST CSF 2.0 provides a flexible, risk-based approach for organizations of all sizes to manage cybersecurity risks. Released February 26, 2024, it represents an evolution from the original framework with enhanced focus on governance and supply chain considerations
