## What is cyber kill chain?
This is a framework that was introduced by Lockheed Martin that breaks down cyberattacks into distinct stages for identification and prevention of cyber intrusions activity.

The model identifies what the adversaries must complete in order to achieve their objective.

- Allows security professionals to provide structured defense by understanding the phases to provide defenses against them and to be ahead.
- Countering Advanced threats
	- Particularly advanced persistent threats which are sophisticated and long term attacks that require  a detailed understanding of progression.
- A Common language for Threat intelligence so Analysts can share intelligence between others.

Used to describe the attack lifecycle that a threat actor carries out.
### 7 Different stages:
- Recon - Gathering information
- Weaponize - creating malware or exploit
- Deliver - The stage where the payload or exploit is delivered to the target.
- Exploit - The stage when the exploit is delivered 
- Install - Rootkits, Droppers, Backdoors(persistence and exfiltration)
- C&C - command and control 
- Action - The objective of the attack (ransom, public statement, trade secrets)
