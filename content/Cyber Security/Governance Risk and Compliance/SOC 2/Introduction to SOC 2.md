
## What is SOC2?
- SOC 2 compliance refers to meeting the requirements of a SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2) audit.
- This is a framework developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA) to evaluate how well service organizations manage customer data and protect it from unauthorized access.

There are 5 main Trust service criteria, think of this as 5 main areas,
- Security 
- Availability 
- Processing Integrity 
- Confidentiality 
- Privacy
