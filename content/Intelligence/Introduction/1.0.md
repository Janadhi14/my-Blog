## What is intelligence?
Intelligence transforms raw information into **understanding** that helps people make better decisions, especially in uncertain or complex situations
## How can we use intelligence?
1. **Support Decision Making**  
    Intelligence is _decision-driven_—it exists to help leaders (e.g., executives, public officials, military commanders) choose the best course of action based on anticipated risks or opportunities.
    
2. **Predict the Future**  
    Intelligence helps forecast possible outcomes by providing _predictive assessments_. This enables proactive action, such as avoiding threats or exploiting opportunities.
    
3. **Improve Timing**  
    _Timely intelligence_ ensures decisions are made at the right moment—before threats escalate or opportunities are missed.
    
4. **Identify Risks and Opportunities**  
    Intelligence analysis helps uncover hidden patterns or trends, often using techniques like scenario building, hypothesis testing, and structured analytical methods.
    
5. **Coordinate Action Across Agencies**  
    It enables different teams, departments, or even countries to work together using a shared understanding, especially during crises or complex operations (like a pandemic response or cybersecurity threats).
    
6. **Enhance Security and Ethics**  
    Intelligence supports national security, law enforcement, and regulatory enforcement while requiring high ethical standards—respecting privacy, legality, and trust.
    
7. **Guide Resource Allocation**  
    It helps organisations decide how to allocate people, time, and money efficiently based on accurate, objective analysis of what's happening and what might happen.
