The **SC-900: Microsoft Security, Compliance, and Identity Fundamentals** exam is an entry-level certification focusing on Microsoft’s security, compliance, and identity (SCI) solutions. It is designed for those who want to gain foundational knowledge in cybersecurity, especially within Microsoft environments.

### **Key Topics Covered:**

1. **Security, Compliance, and Identity Concepts (10-15%)**
    
    - Principles of zero trust
        
    - Shared responsibility model
        
    - Authentication vs. authorization
        
    - Identity protection and governance
        
2. **Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) (25-30%)**
    
    - Identity solutions and authentication methods
        
    - Conditional Access policies
        
    - Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
        
    - Identity governance (Privileged Identity Management, Access Reviews)
        
3. **Microsoft Security Solutions (25-30%)**
    
    - Microsoft Defender solutions (Defender for Endpoint, Defender for Cloud, Defender for Office 365)
        
    - Microsoft Sentinel (SIEM)
        
    - Threat detection and response
        
4. **Microsoft Compliance and Risk Solutions (25-30%)**
    
    - Microsoft Purview (Compliance Manager, Insider Risk Management, Information Protection)
        
    - Data loss prevention (DLP)
        
    - eDiscovery and audit solutions
        

### **Exam Details:**

- **Format:** Multiple-choice and multiple-response questions
    
- **Duration:** ~45-60 minutes
    
- **Passing Score:** ~700/1000
    
- **Cost:** ~$99 USD
