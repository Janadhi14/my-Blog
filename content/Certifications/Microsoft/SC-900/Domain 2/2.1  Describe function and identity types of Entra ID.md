## Describe microsoft Entra ID
- Entra ID is microsoft's cloud-based identity and access management service that functions to
	- help employees sign in and access resources like:
	- **internal resources** such as apps on your corporate network or custom cloud apps
	- **external resources** such as 365 , the azure portal and many SaaS apps
## Describe types of identities 
Device:
- A device is a piece of hardware such as. a mobile device, laptops, servers or printer. Can be set up in different ways in Entra, including:
- Entra registered device
	- Devices can be windows 10 or 11 , IOS, Android, or macOS devices 
- Entra joined 
	- Deices exist only in the cloud, Entra joined devices are owned by an organization and signed in with their Entra account 
- Entra Hybrid joined 
	- Devices can be Windows 10 and 11, or Windows Server 2016, or newer. They are owned by an organization and signed in with an active directory domain services account 
## External identities 
B2B collaboration
- Enable external users to use their preferred identity to sign into your Microsoft or other enterprise applications(SaaA apps, custom-developed apps, ect)
B2B direct connect 
- Establish a mutual 2 way trust with another Entra ID organisation for seamless collaboration
- useful for heavy, dail collaboration with close business partner s
B2C - Business to consumer 
- Publish modern SaaS aops or custom-developed apps
Entra ID multi-tenant organization
- Collaboration with multiple tenants in a single Entra ID organisation via cross-tenant synchronization which is good or conglomerates , mergers, multi-cloud, dept/test/staging tenants
B2B Collaboration(Work Entra ID accounts)
B2C Access Management (social personal identities  )

## What is hybrid Identity?
- This is a phrase used to describe integration of on premises and cloudbased IT infrastruectuers 
- higher security (onprem controls and cloud features)
- Greater productivity - users have a single set of crednentials to access both on-premises and coud-based resources, reducing time spend on authentication prompt 
- Simplified management - allows organisations to manage their on-premises and cloud-based identites from a single locaton, reducing the IT support burden
