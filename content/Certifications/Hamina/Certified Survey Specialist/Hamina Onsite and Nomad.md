## Hamina Onsite: A Comprehensive Site Survey Solution
Hamina Onsite is a powerful and user-friendly application designed to facilitate precise Wi-Fi site surveys. It integrates seamlessly with specialized hardware like the Oscium Nomad to provide a comprehensive solution for Wi-Fi network validation and analysis. Key features and capabilities of Hamina Onsite include:

*   **Ease of Use:** Hamina Onsite is built with a focus on learn-as-you-go usability, making it accessible for both experienced professionals and those new to site surveying.
*   **Multiple Survey Modes:** It supports various site survey modes, including point, line, and continuous surveys, allowing users to choose the most appropriate method for their specific needs.
*   **Real-Time Heatmaps:** A standout feature is its ability to display live heatmaps of survey results. This provides instant visual feedback on Wi-Fi coverage, signal strength, and other critical metrics as the survey is being conducted, enabling immediate troubleshooting and validation of survey paths.
*   **AI-Assisted Building Modeling:** Hamina Onsite leverages AI to assist with building modeling, which can streamline the process of creating accurate floor plans for survey purposes.
*   **Integration with Hamina Planner:** It combines the ease-of-use of Hamina Planner, a design tool, with the simplicity of the Oscium Nomad, creating a cohesive and efficient site survey experience.
*   **Client View:** Hamina Onsite offers a unique Client View feature, allowing users to simulate and analyze how various client devices will perform on the network.
*   **Spectrum Analysis Integration:** It supports real-time spectrum analysis when paired with compatible hardware like the Oscium Wi-Spy Lucid and Oscium WiPry Clarity, helping to identify and mitigate non-Wi-Fi interference.

## Oscium Nomad: The Ideal Companion for Hamina Onsite
The Oscium Nomad is a portable, high-performance Wi-Fi measurement device specifically designed to work in conjunction with Hamina Onsite for Wi-Fi network validation and analysis. Its specifications make it an excellent tool for professional site surveys:

*   **Light and Compact:** The Nomad's design prioritizes portability, making it easy to carry and use during extensive site surveys.
*   **Multiple Wi-Fi 6E Radios:** It features four Wi-Fi 6E radios, each with two spatial streams. This advanced capability allows for comprehensive scanning across the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and the newly available 6 GHz bands, which is crucial for surveying modern Wi-Fi 6E networks.
*   **Extended Battery Life:** With approximately 4 hours of continuous surveying capability and super-fast charging, the Nomad is built for sustained use in the field.
*   **Comprehensive Data Collection:** The Nomad is capable of collecting extensive data for Wi-Fi network validation, including signal strength, noise, and interference information.
*   **Connectivity:** It includes a USB-C Power Delivery charging port, a USB-C Data port, and a USB-A Spectrum port, offering versatile connectivity options.
*   **Antennas:** The device is equipped with 8 antennas, contributing to its robust data collection capabilities.

Together, Hamina Onsite and the Oscium Nomad provide a powerful and efficient solution for Wi-Fi professionals to conduct accurate site surveys, validate network designs, and troubleshoot wireless performance issues.
