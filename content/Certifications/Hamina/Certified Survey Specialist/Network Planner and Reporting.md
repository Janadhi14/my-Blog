## Detailed Network Planner – Survey Analysis
Hamina Network Planner is a powerful tool that extends beyond just design, offering comprehensive capabilities for survey analysis. After conducting a site survey (using Hamina Onsite and Nomad, or importing data from other sources), the Network Planner allows for in-depth analysis of the collected data. This analysis is crucial for validating network performance, identifying issues, and optimizing the wireless environment.

Key aspects of survey analysis in Hamina Network Planner include:

*   **Heatmap Visualization:** The core of survey analysis lies in visualizing the collected data through various heatmaps. These heatmaps graphically represent metrics like signal strength (RSSI), signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), noise floor, channel interference, and data rates, overlaid on the floor plan. This visual representation makes it easy to identify areas of good coverage, weak signals, or high interference.
*   **Live Network Analysis:** Hamina Network Planner, especially with its 'Plus' version and 'Live View' feature, can visualize live network coverage, interference, and capacity. This allows for real-time monitoring and analysis of the network's performance as it operates.
*   **Troubleshooting:** By analyzing the survey data, network engineers can pinpoint the root causes of connectivity issues, performance bottlenecks, and user complaints. For example, a heatmap showing low SNR in a specific area might indicate a high noise floor or excessive interference.
*   **Validation against Requirements:** The collected survey data can be compared against the initial design requirements and performance objectives. This helps in confirming whether the deployed network meets the desired standards.
*   **Client Behavior Simulation:** Features like 'Client View' allow simulating how different client devices would behave on the network, including their roaming patterns and band preferences, which aids in understanding the user experience.

## Detailed Color Code "My APs"
The "Color Code 'My APs'" feature in Hamina Network Planner is a visualization tool that helps in understanding the coverage and influence of your own access points (APs) within the network design or survey. This feature is particularly useful for:

*   **Identifying AP Coverage Areas:** By assigning a unique color to the coverage area of each of your APs, you can quickly visualize which AP is serving which part of the building. This helps in identifying coverage overlap and potential gaps.
*   **Optimizing AP Placement:** When designing or refining a network, this visualization can help in adjusting AP locations to ensure balanced coverage and to avoid areas where clients might struggle to associate with a preferred AP.
*   **Troubleshooting Roaming Issues:** If clients are not roaming effectively, visualizing the coverage of individual APs can help identify areas where signal overlap is insufficient or where a client might be 


sticking to a distant AP.
*   **Channel Planning:** This feature can also be used in conjunction with channel planning to ensure that APs with overlapping coverage are assigned non-overlapping channels to minimize co-channel interference.

## Detailed Reporting Capabilities
Hamina Network Planner offers robust reporting capabilities, allowing users to generate comprehensive and customizable reports of their wireless network designs and survey results. These reports are essential for documentation, client communication, and project handover.

Key features of Hamina's reporting include:

*   **Customizable Reports:** Users can often select which data points, heatmaps, and visualizations to include in their reports, tailoring the content to specific audiences or project requirements. This allows for creating reports for different stakeholders, such as technical teams, management, or clients.
*   **Multiple Report Formats:** Hamina typically supports various report formats, including:
    *   **PDF Reports:** For traditional, static documentation that can be easily printed or shared.
    *   **Interactive Online Reports:** These are dynamic web links that allow recipients to explore the network design and survey data interactively in a browser. This provides a richer experience than static PDFs, enabling stakeholders to zoom, pan, and toggle different layers of information.
*   **Detailed Documentation:** Reports include detailed information about access point placement, configuration, signal strength, noise, interference, and other critical metrics. They can also incorporate survey notes, photos, and other contextual information collected during the project.
*   **Project Sharing:** Hamina often integrates project sharing features, allowing teams to collaborate on designs and reports. This ensures that all team members have access to the latest project data and can contribute to the reporting process.
*   **Compliance and Validation:** Reports serve as official documentation that the wireless network has been designed and validated according to specific requirements and industry best practices.

By providing both static and interactive reporting options, Hamina ensures that network professionals can effectively communicate their findings and the value of their work to a wide range of audiences.
